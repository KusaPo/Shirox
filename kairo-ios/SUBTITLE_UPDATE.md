# Toggleable captions and playback refresh regression

The player now has a CC menu with Off and the source's subtitle tracks. ReAnime reads actual ASS/SSA, SRT, and WebVTT files using source-required headers. Captions use the player's episode clock, including seeking and speed changes; switching Off immediately clears the caption view. Native embedded subtitle groups use AVPlayerItem selection. ReAnime external captions use a label inside AVPlayerViewController's content overlay, so they remain independent of the hideable controls. Plain dialogue is preserved; ASS drawing/font/layout effects are intentionally omitted. External captions are not available in Picture in Picture or remote AirPlay.

Clean HD-1/HD-2 video is preferred instead of H-SUB, since baked-in captions cannot be toggled. H-SUB is identified in the menu. Existing H-SUB downloads still contain captions in their pixels; obtain a clean-video download to remove those captions.

New background downloads include English caption documents and the currently preferred track when available. Caption fetching is optional: its failure does not interrupt video downloads. Documents are published beside the completed episode and need no network afterward. Older downloads can discover/fetch captions on first online playback and cache the selected track without downloading the video again. Direct-file sidecars are unique per media filename to avoid crossing episodes.

Regression coverage includes ASS commas/Unicode/line breaks, drawing rejection, SRT/VTT timing, overlapping cues, backwards seeking, offline document isolation, CC Off/on without a network, and avoiding repeated assignment of the same AVPlayer during UI refreshes.

Audio investigation: the reported ticking occurs both online and offline. The reference Solo Leveling S2 episode 1 English audio decoded for about 30 seconds through the source's real playlist, wrapped segments, and AES path with no FFmpeg decoder errors, clipping, or large boundary jumps. This does not reproduce or rule out the user's affected episode. A simulator regression reproduced 20 reattachments for 20 UI refreshes before the fix. A separate reproducible defect was found in NativePlayer: progress updates unconditionally assigned the same AVPlayer to AVPlayerViewController. Removing those repeated reattachments is a playback-stability fix; an audible ticking fix cannot be confirmed without the exact affected episode or a short recording.
