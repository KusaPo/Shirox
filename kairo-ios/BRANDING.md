# Kairo branding

Selected direction: concept A, K / Play. Purple K, lilac upper arm, white play symbol, dark ink background.

The production icon was created with the built-in image generation tool using the approved concept as the edit reference. It was resized to 1024 × 1024 for Xcode without changing the design. The PNG is opaque; iOS applies the corner mask.

Asset paths:
- `Kairo/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/KairoIcon.png`
- `Kairo/Resources/Assets.xcassets/KairoLogo.imageset/KairoIcon.png`

Final generation prompt:

> Extract and faithfully refine only the bottom-left dark-background app icon from the approved Kairo concept A into a standalone production iOS app icon. Preserve the rounded K monogram, rich violet main K, pale lilac upper-right stroke, and white right-pointing play triangle. Center the symbol at approximately 60% of the canvas width, with equal optical padding. Use an opaque dark ink background (#16121F) extending to all square corners and edges; no rounded outer mask. No wordmark, labels, dividers, board, phone, borders or other text. Clean vector-like edges, restrained violet shading, 1024 × 1024 target PNG. Preserve the chosen design.
